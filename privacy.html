The short version: This website has no accounts, no ads, no advertising trackers and no cookies. It measures visits with Plausible, a privacy-first analytics service that is cookie-free and does not build a profile of you or follow you across other websites. If — and only if — you type your email into the newsletter box, that address is stored by Buttondown so new-release emails can be sent, and you can unsubscribe in one click. The contact form never reaches a server: it opens WhatsApp on your own device with the message pre-filled, and you decide whether to press send. Books are bought on Amazon, never here.

1. Information We Collect

This website (“the Site”) is a static informational website about the author Sufi Ilham (also known as MD Naiyer Alam) and his published books. It is designed to collect as little information as technically possible. Specifically:

  • No account registration. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to set.
  • No advertising, profiling or cross-site tracking. No ad pixels, no remarketing tags, no data brokers, no fingerprinting.
  • No payment processing. All purchases happen on Amazon, never here.
  • Aggregate analytics only — anonymous, cookie-free page-view counts, described in section 5.
  • Your email address, only if you choose to give it to the newsletter box, described in section 4.

Everything else reaches the author only when you deliberately send it — by phone, by WhatsApp, or through the contact form which opens WhatsApp on your device.

2. How Information Is Used

If you contact the author directly, the details you provide (such as your name, phone number and the content of your message) are used solely to reply to your enquiry and, where relevant, to keep a record of the correspondence. If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is used solely to tell you when a new book is published. Your information is never sold, rented, published, or shared for marketing purposes, and it is never used to make automated decisions about you.

3. Contact Form & WhatsApp

The contact form on this Site has no backend server. When you press send, your browser assembles the text you typed into a WhatsApp link and opens WhatsApp. Nothing is transmitted to or stored by this website at any point, and the text never touches a server belonging to the author.

Once your message is delivered through WhatsApp or a phone call, that communication is governed by the privacy policy of the relevant provider (for WhatsApp, Meta Platforms) and by your mobile network operator. Please review their policies for details of how they handle your data.

4. Newsletter

The Site offers an optional new-release newsletter. The subscription box is provided by Buttondown (Buttondown, LLC, United States), and submitting it sends your email address to Buttondown, where it is stored so that the newsletter can be delivered.

  • What is collected: the email address you type, plus the technical metadata Buttondown records to deliver mail and prevent abuse (for example the time you subscribed).
  • What it is used for: a short email when a new book goes live. Nothing else. No third-party advertising is ever sent to the list.
  • Opting out: every email contains a one-click unsubscribe link. You may also ask the author directly and your address will be removed.
  • Legal basis (where the GDPR applies): your consent, given by submitting the form, which you may withdraw at any time.

Buttondown's own handling of your data is described in its privacy policy. If you never enter an email address, no address is ever collected.

5. Analytics

To understand which books and pages readers are interested in, the Site uses Plausible Analytics (Plausible Insights OÜ, Estonia, with data processed and stored in the European Union). Plausible was chosen specifically because it is privacy-first:

  • It sets no cookies and uses no other persistent identifier on your device.
  • It does not collect personal data and does not track you across websites, sessions or devices.
  • It records only aggregate, non-identifying measurements: page URL, referring site, browser, operating system, device type, and country. Full IP addresses are not stored — they are only used transiently to derive an anonymous, daily-rotating hash.
  • Because no personal data is stored and no cookies are set, no cookie-consent banner is required.

The Site uses the “outbound links” extension, which additionally counts how many times a link to Amazon or another external site was clicked. This is a pure counter — it is not tied to you as an individual. You can read Plausible's data policy for full technical detail. Setting your browser's “Do Not Track” signal, or using any common content blocker, will prevent the script from loading; the Site works perfectly without it.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

This Site sets no cookies — none of its own, and none from analytics. It does not fingerprint your device and runs no advertising pixels.

It does use a small amount of your browser's local storage to remember your own display preferences, so the Site behaves the way you left it. This information stays on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is not used to identify or profile you:

  • si_lang — which of the four languages (English, हिन्दी, اردو, Hinglish) you selected.
  • si_music — whether you switched the ambient soundtrack on or off.

You can erase both at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings; the Site will simply return to its defaults.

7. Hosting & Server Logs

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like virtually all web hosts, GitHub's servers may automatically record standard technical information — such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and the date and time of the request — in security and performance logs. These logs are generated by the hosting infrastructure, not by the author, who has no access to them. GitHub's practices are described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Amazon & Purchases

All books are sold and fulfilled by Amazon. Any order you place — including payment details, delivery address and account information — is handled entirely by Amazon under Amazon's Privacy Notice. The author never sees or stores your payment information. Prices, availability and Kindle Unlimited status shown on this Site are indicative and may change at any time on Amazon.

Some book covers on this Site are loaded directly from Amazon's image servers, which means your browser makes a request to Amazon when a page displays them. Following any external link takes you off this Site, after which the destination's own privacy policy and terms apply. The author is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party websites.

10. How Long We Keep It

  • Newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe or ask to be removed, after which the address is deleted.
  • Messages you send: kept only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any reasonable follow-up.
  • Analytics: retained by Plausible in aggregate, non-identifying form only.
  • Server logs: held by GitHub under its own retention schedule; the author has no control over, or access to, them.

11. Data Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. Because it holds no user database of its own, there is no stored personal data on this Site that could be exposed in a breach. Newsletter addresses are held by Buttondown under its own security programme, and correspondence held by the author is kept on password-protected personal devices. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

12. Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. No information is knowingly collected from children, and the newsletter should not be subscribed to by a child. If you believe a child has sent personal information or subscribed, please make contact so it can be deleted.

13. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, or the California Consumer Privacy Act — including the right to access, correct, delete, port or restrict the use of your personal data, to withdraw consent, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (this Site does neither), and to be free from discrimination for exercising any of them.

Because this Site itself stores no personal data, such requests will normally concern either your newsletter subscription or messages you have sent the author directly. To exercise any right, contact +91 62017 57330 or use the contact page; your request will be honoured promptly and normally within 30 days, free of charge. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

14. International Visitors

The Site is operated from India and can be viewed worldwide. The service providers listed in section 8 are located in the European Union and the United States, so information handled by them may be processed outside your country. By using the Site you understand that any message you choose to send may be received and read in India.

15. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in practice or law. The revision date at the top of the page will always show when it was last changed, and material changes will be made clear there. Continued use of the Site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
