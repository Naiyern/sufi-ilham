The short version: This website does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no newsletter database, no analytics scripts and no advertising trackers. The contact form does not send anything to a server — it simply opens WhatsApp on your own device with your message pre-filled, and you choose whether to press send.

1. Information We Collect

This website ("the Site") is a static informational website about the author Sufi Ilham (also known as MD Naiyer Alam) and his published books. It is designed to collect as little information as technically possible.

  • No account registration. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No newsletter list. No email addresses are stored by this Site.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers are embedded in these pages.
  • No payment processing. All purchases happen on Amazon, never here.

Information reaches the author only when you choose to send it — by phone, by WhatsApp, or by using the contact form which opens WhatsApp on your device.

2. How Information Is Used

If you contact the author directly, the details you provide (such as your name, phone number and the content of your message) are used solely to reply to your enquiry and, where relevant, to keep a record of the correspondence. Your information is never sold, rented, published or shared for marketing purposes.

3. The Contact Form & WhatsApp

The contact form on this Site has no backend server. When you press send, your browser assembles the text you typed into a WhatsApp link and opens WhatsApp. Nothing is transmitted to or stored by this website at any point.

Once your message is delivered through WhatsApp or a phone call, that communication is governed by the privacy policy of the relevant provider (for WhatsApp, Meta Platforms) and by your mobile network operator. Please review their policies for details of how they handle your data.

4. Cookies & Tracking

This Site sets no cookies of its own. It does not use local storage to profile you, does not fingerprint your device, and does not run advertising or analytics pixels. Because no tracking cookies are used, no cookie-consent banner is required.

5. Hosting & Server Logs

Like virtually all websites, the Site is delivered by a hosting provider whose servers may automatically record standard technical information — such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and the date and time of the request — in security and performance logs. These logs are generated by the hosting infrastructure, not by the author, and are used only to keep the service running securely. Please consult your host's documentation for their retention periods.

6. Third-Party Links

The Site links to external websites, most notably Amazon. Once you follow such a link you leave this Site, and the destination's own privacy policy and terms apply. The author is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party websites.

7. Amazon & Purchases

All books are sold and fulfilled by Amazon. Any order you place — including payment details, delivery address and account information — is handled entirely by Amazon under Amazon's Privacy Notice. The author never sees or stores your payment information. Prices, availability and Kindle Unlimited status shown on this Site are indicative and may change at any time on Amazon.

8. Data Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. Because it holds no user database, there is no stored personal data on this Site that could be exposed in a breach. Correspondence held by the author is kept on password-protected personal devices.

9. Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. No information is knowingly collected from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information, please make contact so it can be deleted.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the EU/UK GDPR, or the CCPA — including the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent.

Because this Site stores no personal data, such requests will usually concern messages you have sent the author directly. To exercise any right, contact +91 62017 57330 and your request will be honoured promptly, normally within 30 days.

11. International Visitors

The Site is operated from India and can be viewed worldwide. By using it you understand that any message you choose to send may be received and read in India.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in practice or law. The revision date at the top of the page will always show when it was last changed. Continued use of the Site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
