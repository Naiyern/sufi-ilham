# Book-detail override — Cover / Story / Editions

Apply on top of `MASTER.md` for `/books/*`.

## Page structure

1. Open with a dark, book-jacket hero: breadcrumb, real cover, publication/free status, title and transliteration when available, short subtitle or verified excerpt, compact facts and direct reading/store actions.
2. Use the book's existing series accent when it belongs to a series; otherwise use the Sufi Ilham vermilion. Keep the dark field and warm-paper reading area shared across books.
3. Put the complete description in a readable paper section with a narrow measure. Keep edition links distinct and explicit; retailer pricing belongs to the retailer.
4. For free books, make “Read free online” the primary action and retain chapter links/reader preferences already provided by the reader.
5. Offer related books from the same series or genre, not invented endorsements or ratings.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Keep store links as real links and reader controls as real buttons/links; dialogs remain keyboard-operable.
- Preserve book language direction (`dir`) and do not force Latin transliterations into the book title.
- On small screens, stack cover and copy; never shrink the primary action to an icon.
- Maintain contrast for the dynamic series accents against the ink cover, visible focus, and reduced-motion behavior.
