# Homepage override — Ink Cover / Living Catalogue

Apply this page-level direction on top of `MASTER.md`.

## Visual hierarchy

1. Open straight into the site; do not block first-time visitors with a full-screen welcome gate.
2. Treat the first viewport as a literary book jacket: deep ink background, oversize Instrument Serif author name, restrained vermilion/gold marks, real portrait, and one paper-coloured latest-release slip.
3. Keep the catalogue and editorial sections on warm paper so the dark cover has contrast and the page still feels like a printed archive.
4. Give current releases a clear featured-book card treatment; use catalogue data and existing cover assets only.
5. Make the full catalogue cover-led and searchable, with real book links that can progressively open a details dialog; do not rely on hover-only previews.
6. Keep the discovery path direct: “Discover the latest” and “Browse the archive.” Do not add unsupported reviews, testimonials, awards, or sales claims.

## Interaction

- The fixed masthead remains readable over the dark hero and switches to an equally legible dark mobile drawer.
- The release cards remain two-column at desktop, then stack cleanly at tablet/mobile widths.
- Keep search and filters visible when JavaScript works; retain the unfiltered catalogue as the no-JavaScript fallback.
- Respect the project's reduced-motion and keyboard-focus rules; never reintroduce a blocking intro or autoplay sound.

## Review checklist

Review at 375px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px. Check contrast across the ink cover/paper card boundary, no horizontal overflow, all calls to action visible, release titles untruncated, and the portrait's face not cropped by the image frame.
