Common Questions

Before you write

Where can I buy the books?

All eight titles are on Amazon — Amazon.com for international readers and Amazon.in for India. Four titles are free to read with Kindle Unlimited.

Do you accept interview or podcast requests?

Yes. Choose "Interview Request" in the form above or message the number directly, and include your outlet, format and preferred dates.

Can I order in bulk for a school, library or bookstore?

Absolutely. Get in touch with the quantity and destination and arrangements can be made through Amazon or directly.

Are translation rights available?

Enquiries about translation and foreign-language editions are welcome. Select "Translation Rights" and include the language and territory.

Will you read my manuscript?

Unsolicited manuscripts cannot be reviewed or returned, but thoughtful questions about writing and the ideas in the books are always welcome.
