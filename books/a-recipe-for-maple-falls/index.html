Theodore “The Palate” Sinclair was once the most feared restaurant critic on the Eastern Seaboard. Now he’s a man with a ruined reputation, a grandmother’s house to sell, and no idea what comes next. Then he walks into the Frosted Maple bakery, orders the pear cardamom pie, and everything changes — including, reluctantly, him.

Rosie Okafor has spent twenty years building Maple Falls’ favourite bakery on one iron rule: never mix business and pleasure. But the quiet stranger with the city coat and the sad eyes keeps coming back, and he tastes her food like a man reading a love letter.

There’s just one problem: thirty years ago, a young critic wrote three sentences that destroyed Rosie’s first patisserie — and cost her everything. Now she’s falling for the man who wrote them.

When a magazine feature threatens to expose the town’s newest secret, he must choose: run again, or finally write the only honest review of his life.
