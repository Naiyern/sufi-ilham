Neris Pell still will not declare him. Calden Rue said yes without a box to tick, and the gold seal at his heel does not care which city they are in — the bond follows, the Crown follows, and a debt can outrun its receipt.

Now the woman who taught the Tithe its manners wants her collector back. Calden is hers by old rules, old debts, and a claim Neris has no intention of honouring. But Neris is not the only thing being hunted: the forger bound to Calden is an asset the Crown would rather cut than lose — and someone has decided she is worth retrieving intact.

A cut can arrive before the shadow. A name can become a sentence. And if Neris will not declare Calden, she will declare something else: the gap.

In Isker, shadows arrive late — and sometimes, what arrives late is the thing that kills you.
