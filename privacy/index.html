The short version: This site has no accounts, advertising or payment processing, and does not set cookies. It loads Plausible Analytics only when your browser does not signal Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control; analytics is cookie-free and does not follow you across other sites. The reader saves your display preferences and reading position in your browser’s local storage. Contact and new-release forms create a pre-filled WhatsApp link; nothing is sent to the author unless you choose to send it. Books offered for purchase are sold by Amazon; one Urdu novella can be read here for free.

1. Information We Collect

This website (“the Site”) is a static informational website about the author Sufi Ilham (also known as MD Naiyer Alam), the published catalogue and forthcoming books. It is designed to collectas little information as technically possible. Specifically:

  • No account registration. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to set.
  • No advertising, profiling or cross-site tracking. No ad pixels, no remarketing tags, no data brokers, no fingerprinting.
  • No payment processing. Purchases are handled off-site by Amazon; the Site also provides one Urdu novella to read for free.
  • Aggregate analytics only — anonymous, cookie-free page-view counts, described in section 5.
  • Information you choose to send directly. If you use the new-release request form, your email address is included in a WhatsApp draft. This Site does not receive it; the author receives it only if you choose to send that message.

Everything else reaches the author only when you deliberately send it — by phone, by WhatsApp, or through the contact form which opens WhatsApp on your device.

2. How Information Is Used

If you contact the author directly, the details you provide (such as your name, phone number and the content of your message) are used to reply to your enquiry and, where relevant, to keep a record of the correspondence. If you send a new-release request through WhatsApp, the author may use the email address in that message to respond to your request. Your information is never sold, rented, published, or shared for marketing purposes, and it is never used to make automated decisions about you.

3. Contact Form & WhatsApp

The contact form on this Site has no backend server. When you press send, your browser assembles the text you typed into a WhatsApp link and opens WhatsApp. Nothing is transmitted to or stored by this website at any point, and the text never touches a server belonging to the author.

Once your message is delivered through WhatsApp or a phone call, that communication is governed by the privacy policy of the relevant provider (for WhatsApp, Meta Platforms) and by your mobile network operator. Please review their policies for details of how they handle your data.

4. New-release updates

The Site does not send email newsletters and does not use an email-marketing provider. Submitting the new-release request form opens a WhatsApp link containing the email address and request you typed. WhatsApp receives that link when it opens, but the message is delivered to the author only if you choose to send it in WhatsApp.

  • What the Site collects: nothing from this form. The address is not sent to a server operated by this Site and is not stored here.
  • What happens when the link opens: WhatsApp / Meta receives the pre-filled link and may process it under its own privacy terms. If you press Send, the author receives the address and request in a WhatsApp message.
  • What it is used for: responding to your request for new-release updates. The Site has no automated mailing list or unsubscribe workflow.
  • Legal basis (where the GDPR applies): by opening the link and choosing to send the message, you choose to share its contents with the author through WhatsApp.

The email field is required for the pre-filled release-request flow. If you use the direct WhatsApp link instead, you decide what, if anything, to include. The Site itself does not collect or retain the address.

5. Analytics

To understand which books and pages readers are interested in, the Site usesPlausible Analytics (Plausible Insights OÜ, Estonia, with data processed and stored in the European Union). Plausible was chosen specifically because it is privacy-first:

  • It sets no cookies and uses no other persistent identifier on your device.
  • It does not use a persistent identifier or follow you across other websites, sessions or devices.
  • It records only aggregate, non-identifying measurements: page URL, referring site, browser, operating system, device type, and country. Full IP addresses are not stored — they are only used transiently to derive an anonymous, daily-rotating hash.
  • The Site itself sets no cookies; the local-storage preferences used by the reader are described in section 6.

The Site uses Plausible’s outbound-links extension to count external-link clicks. It also records an Amazon Click event with the book title and destination URL when the Amazon hand-off opens, plus a New Release Request event when that form is submitted. These events do not include your email address, name or message text. You can read Plausible’s data policy for technical detail. Before adding the analytics script, this Site checks Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control; if either is enabled, it does not request the script. A content blocker may also block analytics. All other site features work without it.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

This Site sets no cookies — none of its own, and none from analytics. It does not fingerprint your device and runs no advertising pixels.

The Site uses your browser’s local storage to remember display and reading choices. These values stay in this browser on this device, are not sent to the author, and are not used to identify or profile you:

  • si_music — whether the ambient soundtrack is on or off.
  • theme — the site-wide light or dark theme.
  • reader:prefs — the reader’s chosen theme, page width and font size.
  • reader:<book-slug> — your saved scroll position in that book’s online reader, so you can resume where you left off.

These entries are created only as you use the relevant controls or reader. You can erase them at any time by clearing this site’s local storage in your browser settings; the preferences and saved positions will then reset.

7. Hosting & Server Logs

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like virtually all web hosts, GitHub's servers may automatically record standard technical information — such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and the date and time of the request — in security and performance logs. These logs are generated by the hosting infrastructure, not by the author, who has no access to them. GitHub's practices are described in theGitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Amazon & Purchases

Books offered for purchase are sold and fulfilled by Amazon. The Site also provides one free Urdu novella to read online; no payment is processed here. Any Amazon order — including payment details, delivery address and account information — is handled by Amazon under Amazon’s Privacy Notice. The author never sees or stores your payment information. Prices, availability and Kindle Unlimited status shown on this Site are indicative and may change at any time on Amazon.

Some book covers on this Site are loaded directly from Amazon's image servers, which means your browser makes a request to Amazon when a page displays them. Following any external link takes you off this Site, after which the destination's own privacy policy and terms apply. The author is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party websites.

10. How Long We Keep It

  • Messages you send: kept only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any reasonable follow-up. A new-release request is treated as a message, not an automatic subscription.
  • Analytics: retained by Plausible in aggregate, non-identifying form only.
  • Server logs: held by GitHub under its own retention schedule; the author has no control over, or access to, them.

11. Data Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. Because it holds no user database of its own, it does not retain personal data that could be exposed from a Site database. Messages you choose to send are handled by WhatsApp and may be kept by the author on password-protected personal devices. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

12. Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. No information is knowingly collected from children. Children should not send personal information through the contact or new-release request forms. If you believe a child has sent personal information, please make contact so it can be addressed.

13. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, or the California Consumer Privacy Act — including the right toaccess, correct, delete, port or restrict the use of your personal data, towithdraw consent, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (this Site does neither), and to be free from discrimination for exercising any of them.

The Site does not receive the local-storage preferences described above. Requests will most often concern messages you have sent the author directly. To exercise any right, contact+91 62017 57330 or use the contact page; your request will be honoured promptly and normally within 30 days, free of charge. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

14. International Visitors

The Site is operated from India and can be viewed worldwide. Its providers may process request data in the EU, the United States and other countries, depending on the service and its infrastructure. Any message you choose to send to the author will be received in India.

15. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in practice or law. The revision date at the top of the page will always show when it was last changed, and material changes will be made clear there. Continued use of the Site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
